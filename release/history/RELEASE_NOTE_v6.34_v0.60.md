# Grandis Legacy VS AI v6.34 / Tutorial v0.60

- Enhances local AI decision-making for active Class Abilities and Racial Traits without changing the underlying card rules.
- Aurex now evaluates its same-turn Attack/Tactical options during Deploy. If Aurex has no such planned card use and can pay, it uses its active healing Class Ability on the most damaged legal ally.
- Quick Reload and Rapid Chamber now default to the redraw when legal, preserving Actual Draw counting and draw-scaling interactions.
- Mana Surge and Arcane Surge now default ON when affordable and useful, while avoiding unnecessary bonus-damage overkill.
- Racial AI now uses Human Ambition at 2 or fewer cards in Hand, Ancestral Focus when +2 Shards unlock a Battle Attack, Primal Strike as a 20-HP finisher, Dragon Scale / Second Chance for 30+ incoming damage or low-HP danger, and Stoneblood whenever legally available.
- Tutorial shares the upgraded local AI runtime while preserving the current Shard Deck / Shard Pool teaching flow and Playtest Lab v0.14 gameplay authority.
